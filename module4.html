Module 4 — Preventing Retaliation & Psychological Safety

Goal: Reduce fear of retaliation and build a culture of speaking up.

Signals to Watch

  • Shifts in assignments, ratings, or access shortly after a complaint
  • Social exclusion or negative remarks about complaint activity

Leader Habits

  • Reiterate no‑retaliation standards in writing
  • Offer anonymous consult channels
  • Model curiosity and respect in team forums
