Module 3 — ADR & Mediation Skills

Goal: Apply ADR within timelines; communicate clearly; and record outcomes.

Key Points

  • Initiate ADR within 15 calendar days from initial concern
  • Set ground rules; ensure confidentiality
  • Focus on interests, not positions; explore options

Action

  • Prepare a neutral opening statement
  • Document agreements and next steps immediately
