Module 2 — Leadership Responsibility & Early Resolution

Goal: Empower supervisors to address concerns promptly and document actions professionally.

Leader‑First Model

  • Assess within 2 business days; document facts
  • Hold a respectful conversation using neutral language
  • If unresolved, consult EEO; consider ADR

Practice Script

“Thank you for telling me. I want to understand what happened, how it’s affecting you, and what a good resolution looks like. I’ll follow up by <date>.”

Action

  • Use the Leadership Resolution Record to log steps taken.
  • Schedule a follow‑up within 5 business days.
